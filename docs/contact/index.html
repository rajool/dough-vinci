Questions

How do I order delivery?

Find us on Uber Eats, DoorDash, SkipTheDishes or Fantuan. We don’t take payment on this site.

Can I eat in?

Yes. We have seating, so you can eat in with us, pick up your order or have it delivered.

Can I pick up my order?

Yes. Choose pickup in any of the delivery apps, or call us and collect it at the counter.

Is there parking?

Yes. Free customer parking inside the building, on the ground floor.

Is your food halal?

Yes. All our meat is halal, with halal certification by the BC Muslim Association (BCMA). Our pepperoni, salami, sausage and bresaola are beef.

Do you have vegetarian options?

Yes: the Mona Lisa and Mushroom & Balsamic pizzas, the Veggie Burger, three of our four pastas, most sides and both desserts.

How do I check allergens?

Every dish lists its allergens and calories. Our kitchen is shared, so tell us about severe allergies.

Can you handle large orders?

Yes. Call us a day ahead for office lunches, parties and team events.

When do you open?

We plan to open in early December 2026. The exact day goes up here and on the delivery apps before the first order.

Feeding a crowd?

Pizzas by the stack, smashburgers by the tray. Tell us the date and headcount, and we’ll plan the order for your office, party or team.
