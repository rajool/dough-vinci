Good to know

Questions

How do I order delivery?

Find us on Uber Eats, DoorDash, SkipTheDishes or Fantuan. We don’t take payment on this site.

Can I pick up my order?

Yes. Choose pickup in any of the delivery apps, or call us and collect it at the counter.

Do you have vegetarian options?

Yes: the Mona Lisa and Il Giardino pizzas, the Il Verde burger, and most sides and desserts.

How do I check allergens?

Every dish lists its allergens and calories. Our kitchen is shared, so tell us about severe allergies.

Can you handle large orders?

Yes. Call us a day ahead for office lunches, parties and team events.

When do you open?

We plan to open on Saturday, December 5. The date is confirmed here and on the delivery apps before the first order.

Catering

Feeding a crowd?

Pizzas by the stack, smashburgers by the tray. Tell us the date and headcount, and we’ll plan the order for your office, party or team.
